## Who to ask (p. 2, p. 17)

- Logos must be obtained through the **Sutter Health Brand Center**. Do not recreate them.
- A new logo, or use by a non-Sutter organization or vendor, needs sign-off from the
  Creative Services and Brand Manager, Strategic Marketing. In 2013 that was David Koszka,
  (916) 286-6789, koszkad@sutterhealth.org.
- Naming questions went to the VP of Strategic Marketing (Tracy Murphy in 2013).
- HQ: Sutter Health, 2200 River Plaza Drive, Sacramento, CA 95833. sutterhealth.org

## Logo / signature (pp. 3–6)

- Signature = plus symbol + "Sutter Health" wordmark + tagline **"We Plus You"**
  (tagline set in Helvetica Neue Bold).
- Allowed renderings only:
  - **Color:** wordmark and two arms of the plus in teal green (PMS 3272); the other
    arms in gray (60% PMS 426); tagline gray (75% PMS 426).
  - **Black/grayscale:** wordmark and arms black; other arms 60% gray; tagline 75% gray.
  - **White (reversed):** only on **black, corporate teal green, or corporate gray**.
- No other colors for region/Affiliate signatures.
- Use the full lockup on every medium, including premium items.
- **Clear space:** one-third the width of the plus symbol (= one arm's width) on all sides.
- The plus symbol must appear with the name and tagline. Exceptions: tiny premium
  items, vehicle or advertising graphics (with the full signature elsewhere), and some
  campus signs.
- Never add design elements to the plus. Never pair another icon with the signature
  (except approved co-branding).
- ® may be omitted when the plus is under 0.5" tall.
- Do not crop into the plus symbol (p. 14).

## Tagline (pp. 4–6)

- "We Plus You" is part of the lockup on all communications except signage.
- No other taglines or variations are approved.
- With an Affiliate (e.g. a hospital) signature, the tagline color matches the
  Sutter Health wordmark. Drop the tagline when it would be too small to read.

## Affiliate signatures (pp. 5–6), e.g. California Pacific Medical Center

- Layout: plus symbol, then "Sutter Health" (teal), then the Affiliate name below in
  Helvetica Neue Light (dark gray/black, PMS 426), then "We Plus You". Available in
  one-line and two-line versions.
- Example shown: Sutter Health / Alta Bates Summit Medical Center / We Plus You.
- Affiliate name color: PMS 426 / CMYK 0/0/0/100 / HEX 000000 (color version), or
  0/0/0/50 / HEX 262324 (grayscale version).

## Region, program and department signatures (pp. 7–11)

- **Region signature:** plus + wordmark + region name in Helvetica Neue Light +
  tagline. Example: "Sutter Health Central Valley Region".
- **Regions and their corporations (2013):**
  - Central Valley: Sutter Central Valley Hospitals, Sutter Gould Medical Foundation
  - East Bay: Sutter East Bay Hospitals, Sutter East Bay Medical Foundation
  - Peninsula Coastal: Mills-Peninsula Health Services, Palo Alto Medical Foundation
  - Sacramento Sierra: Sutter Health Sacramento Sierra Region, Sutter Medical Foundation
  - West Bay: Sutter West Bay Hospitals (rest of the list was hidden by the paywall overlay)
- **Alternate region signature:** a vertical rule after the region signature, then a
  list of the affiliated hospitals and foundations.
- **Other organizations:** Sutter Health University, Sutter Select, Sutter Health Plus,
  and Sutter Express Care, each with color and black versions.
- **Major programs** (e.g. Sutter EHR, Sutter Institute for Medical Research),
  **departments** (e.g. Strategy & Business Development) and **Affiliate departments**
  (e.g. Alta Bates Summit / Comprehensive Cancer Center) use the same lockup. The name
  is in Helvetica Neue Light, slightly smaller, with the same colors as Affiliates.

## Co-branding (p. 12)

- An independent program aligned with Sutter or an Affiliate may co-brand on brochures.
  Its logo goes under the Sutter/Affiliate signature or on the same baseline.
  The example pairs Sutter Heart & Vascular Institute with American Heart Association.
- The partner logo is scaled to the Sutter logo and is never larger than it.
- Alternatively, the two logos can go in separate places on the same piece.

## Color palette (p. 13)

is a screened (tint) version of brand gray. Solid 100% gray may be used in a small
area to reverse out a call to action, phone number, or URL. Support colors are only a

### Accessibility check (computed here, not in the guide)

| Pair | Contrast | WCAG AA (4.5:1 body text) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White on `#00AAA6` | 2.88:1 | Fails, even for large text (3:1) |
| Black/`#121212` on `#00AAA6` | 6.5–7.3:1 | Passes |
| White on `#262324` | 15.6:1 | Passes |
| White on `#005695` (PMS 647) | 7.6:1 | Passes |

The guide says to reverse white text out of a teal box. On the web, that fails
contrast at any size. Use dark text on brand teal, or reserve brand teal for large
graphic elements.

## Typography (p. 15)

- Only two typefaces are allowed:
  - **Helvetica Neue LT Std** (primary): 45 Light, 46 Light Italic, 55 Roman,
    56 Italic, 75 Bold, 76 Bold Italic. Condensed is also acceptable.
  - **Minion Pro** (complementary serif): Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic.
- No other typefaces may be used.

## Layout example (p. 14)

- Always use the 2-color logo, with teal dominant and other approved colors as accents.
- Brochure cover: a teal box with white reversed text. Don't place the box over a
  model's face.
- A horizontal rule next to the box can add one palette color. Standard rule weight
  is 6 pt.
- Voice: "clean and simple", "organized, warm and inviting", with health and
  relationships at the heart.

## T-shirts and premium items (p. 16)

- Print colors: PMS 3272 teal green + **PMS 425 gray** (use 425, not 426, on shirts
  and golf balls).
- Embroidery (Isacord thread): 4610 teal green; 0108 light gray (logo); 0132 dark gray
  (shadow, bottom of the plus symbol).
- The tagline may be separated from the logo if the logo appears somewhere on the item.
- Any alteration needed to fit small items needs approval from the marketing manager
  or brand manager.
